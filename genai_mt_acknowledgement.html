    • AI Learning Support & Effectiveness Reflection

    Throughout the development of this project, ChatGPT provides critical assistance with content creation, structural design, and implementation of key page features. The AI provided substantial support across expression, technical layout, and design refinement.

    In content development, ChatGPT helped me draft and polish multiple sections of text. It was especially valuable in shaping the language and tone of the reflective design section, where it offered specific suggestions to ensure clarity and appropriateness using a language checklist. For sections like “Dunhuang Mogao Caves Intro” and “Nine-Colored Deer Story,” it guided enhancements in cultural tone, narrative clarity, and engagement, making the descriptions both informative and expressive.

    In terms of coding, ChatGPT provided testable code samples. In modules like “Image and Text Layout Template,” “Parallel HTML Layouts,” and “Frosted Glass Effect,” I tested the structural feasibility of AI-suggested snippets and adapted them into the site with personalized modifications.

    AI also offered useful suggestions for presenting information. For example, it recommended using tables to compare different cultural elements for better readability. In multilingual content creation, particularly under “Cultural Translation Consultation,” it helped refine English descriptions of artifacts to improve accuracy and consistency.

    I've also facing challenges during the developing process, especially when AI can't provide exact functionality that I want, or the structure does not work well when depolying onto my website. I will then ask AI to provide me with a basic prototype structure, so that I can test if my idea could work on the theoretical level. If it works, then I will try to delopy it onto my website, or provide to AI with the unsatified & unfunctional structures when it not. This process helps me with plenty of unfunctional problems generated by AI, and provide valualbe experience of how I should propose my demands to AI.

    Overall, ChatGPT contributed significantly as a reference and technical aid. I critically evaluated and customized its outputs to align with project goals and ensure originality. This process also deepened my understanding of how to responsibly integrate generative tools into design work.

    Declaration on the Use of AI and Translation Tools

    To ensure the accountability and academic integrity, and to avoid for overuse of generative AI, the statement below is created under the help of AI(mostly for its memory) to declare the proper and transparent usage in this project.

    Purpose and implementation

    Throughout this project, I used ChatGPT and language translation tools in the following ways:

  • Assisting in drafting exhibition descriptions, narrative content, and cultural background integration
  • Structuring visual and interactive design suggestions, such as layout templates and visual effects (e.g., frosted glass, parallel columns)
  • Translating and refining bilingual content to improve accuracy and consistency
  • Providing testable code samples, which I evaluated, adapted, and implemented within the site
  • Recommending clear data organization methods, including tables and comparative layouts for reflective design

    • Potential Ethical Concerns

    The following concerns may arise during the usage of GenAI tools:

  • Originality and Academic Integrity

    • The use of AI may lead to content that is not entirely authored by the individual, potentially raising concerns about plagiarism or academic misconduct—especially if the level of AI involvement is not disclosed clearly.

  • Accountability and Responsibility

    • If AI-generated content includes factual inaccuracies or inappropriate expressions, determining responsibility may be ambiguous. This is particularly critical in design, cultural interpretation, or public-facing projects.

  • Cultural Sensitivity and Contextual Bias

    • AI-generated content may lack adequate cultural awareness, possibly resulting in mistranslations or oversimplified portrayals of culturally significant material.

  • Linguistic Consistency and Voice Control

    • Using AI or translation tools repeatedly may lead to inconsistencies in tone, style, or fluency across a project, affecting the coherence and credibility of the final output.
